Chocolate Fondue

Home

Description

Chocolate fondue is a creamy dessert that is simple to make at home using only chocolate, heavy cream, and fresh fruit.


Ingredients

  • Chocolate
  • Heavy cream
  • Fresh fruit

Steps

  1. Combine the ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Heat the mixture over low heat until it starts to simmer.
  3. Remove from heat and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  4. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
