Lasagna

Home

Description

Lasagna is both a type of pasta consisting of wide ribbons and a dish—sometimes called baked lasagna—made by layering these ribbons with filling and sauce.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Chicken
  • Tomato sauce
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the pasta according to package instructions.
  3. In a separate pan, cook the chicken until browned.
  4. Combine the cooked pasta, chicken, tomato sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
